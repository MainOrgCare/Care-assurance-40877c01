## 3. How We Protect Your Data During AI Processing

### Our Privacy-by-Design Approach

We use a technology called **PII Tokenization** to protect personal information:

1. **Before AI Processing:** Personal details are replaced with random tokens (e.g., "John Smith" becomes "{{PII_abc123_NAME_1}}")

2. **During AI Processing:** Only tokenized (anonymous) data is sent to the AI system

3. **After AI Processing:** Tokens are replaced with original values on our secure servers

**This means:** No identifiable personal information ever leaves our servers or reaches the AI provider.

### What the AI Provider Sees

| What We Send | Example |
|--------------|---------|
| ❌ NOT sent | "Mrs Margaret Jones, DOB 15/03/1940, NHS 123 456 7890" |
| ✅ Sent | "{{PII_NAME_1}}, DOB {{PII_DOB_1}}, NHS {{PII_NHS_1}}" |

## 5. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

### Access Your Data
- View all data we hold about you
- Download your data in a portable format
- **How:** Go to Settings > Privacy > Download My Data

### Request Deletion
- Ask us to delete your personal data
- **How:** Go to Settings > Privacy > Request Deletion

### Withdraw Consent
- Opt out of AI processing at any time
- AI features will fall back to manual mode
- **How:** Go to Settings > Privacy > AI Consent

### Object to Processing
- Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- **How:** Contact our DPO

### Rectification
- Correct inaccurate personal data
- **How:** Edit your profile or contact support

## 9. Automated Decision-Making

Our AI system provides **recommendations only**. It does not:
- Make care decisions automatically
- Diagnose medical conditions
- Prescribe medications
- Replace professional judgment

All AI output must be reviewed and approved by qualified care staff.

**Note:** Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
