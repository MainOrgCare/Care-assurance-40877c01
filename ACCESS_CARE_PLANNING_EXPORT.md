# Access Care Planning Data Export (EMAR)

## Purpose
Provide a consistent, authorized way to export EMAR data for auditing.

## Required Access
- You must be authorized by your organization to access the service user record and export EMAR data.
- Follow your organization’s data protection policy (e.g., approved devices, storage, and retention rules).

## EMAR Export Steps
1. Open Access care Planning and sign in with your authorized account.
2. In the top search bar, search for the service user you want to audit.
3. Open the service user’s dashboard.
4. In the left-side menu, select **Visits**.
5. On the Visits page, select **EMAR** (next to Visits, mid‑screen).
6. Click the **Export** button (left side).

## Data Handling (Security)
- Save the export to an approved, secure location only.
- Do not share the export outside authorized staff or systems.
- Delete the export when the audit is complete, following your retention policy.

## Notes
- If you cannot see EMAR or the Export button, confirm your permissions with your system admin.
